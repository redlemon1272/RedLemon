# Security Policy

## 🍋 Our Commitment to Security

RedLemon is built on the principles of privacy and user trust. We take the security of our application and our users' data very seriously.

## 🛡️ Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability within RedLemon, we appreciate your help in disclosing it to us in a responsible manner. 

**Please DO NOT post security vulnerabilities and exploits on public forums (like Reddit) or the GitHub issue tracker.**

Instead, please report vulnerabilities via one of the following private channels:

1.  **In-App Feedback**: Use the "Send Feedback" button in the Settings menu within the RedLemon app.
2.  **Email**: Send a detailed report to `security@redlemon.live` (fictional for now, user can update).

Please include the following information in your report:
- A description of the vulnerability and its potential impact.
- Steps to reproduce the issue.
- Any suggested fixes or mitigations.

## 🔒 Responsible Disclosure

We ask that you follow the principles of responsible disclosure:
- Give us a reasonable amount of time to fix the issue before making it public.
- Make a good faith effort to avoid privacy violations, destruction of data, and interruption or degradation of our service during your research.

## 🤝 Thank You

The security of RedLemon depends on the community's help. We thank all researchers and users who help us keep RedLemon safe.
